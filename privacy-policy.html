Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the TETHER app handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

TETHER does not collect personal data to company-managed servers.

  • No account is required to use the app.
  • No user data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs are used to profile users.

2. Data Stored in the App

Your relationship and group data is stored locally on your device so you can use the app.

3. iCloud Sync

The only data stored outside the app is data you choose to sync through Apple's iCloud services.

This iCloud sync is used to keep your data available across multiple devices and app installations connected to your Apple ID.

Apple manages iCloud storage and processing under Apple's own privacy terms.

4. Third-Party Services

Other than optional iCloud sync provided by Apple, TETHER does not send your data to third-party services.

5. Your Choices

  • You can choose not to use iCloud sync and keep data only on your device.
  • You can delete app data from within the app or by deleting the app from your device.
  • You can manage iCloud data through your Apple ID and iCloud settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact

If you have privacy questions about TETHER, use the support contact provided on the app's App Store listing.

By using TETHER, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
